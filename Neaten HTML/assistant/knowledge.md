# Neaten Clean

Neaten (also written Neaten Clean) is a domestic and commercial cleaning company based in Manchester. Brand line: "A better feeling, in every space."

Neaten believes cleaning is about more than appearances: homes that feel comfortable, workplaces that make the right impression and spaces that feel properly cared for. Its approach is built on three things:

- **Thoughtful care**: attention to detail and respect for every environment.
- **Dependable standards**: consistent standards, agreed expectations and accountability, visit after visit.
- **Clear communication**: straightforward conversations and no surprises.

Five guiding principles: Considered, Dependable, Fresh, Professional, Reassuring.

Neaten is a new name in Manchester cleaning. Read more on [Our Story](about.html).

# Services

Neaten offers six services in two collections. Each can be enquired about directly. The booking form link below pre-selects that service.

## Neaten Home: cleaning for homes

Thoughtful cleaning for homes that feel comfortable, tidy and cared for, as a regular routine, a deeper reset or a fresh start between occupants.

1. **Regular Domestic Cleaning**: thoughtful, regular cleaning for the home, helping maintain a comfortable, tidy and well-cared-for environment. Well suited to homes that would benefit from dependable, ongoing care on a weekly or fortnightly routine.
   Details: [Regular Domestic Cleaning](services.html#regular-domestic). Enquire: [Book Regular Domestic Cleaning](appointment.html?service=Regular%20Domestic%20Cleaning)

2. **Deep Cleaning**: a more detailed approach for spaces needing additional attention, focused on a fresh, comfortable and carefully maintained environment. Well suited to spaces that need more than a routine clean, or a fresh start before a regular routine begins.
   Details: [Deep Cleaning](services.html#deep-cleaning). Enquire: [Book Deep Cleaning](appointment.html?service=Deep%20Cleaning)

3. **Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning**: cleaning for homes between occupants, whether preparing to move in or getting a property ready to leave, with a focus on a clean and well-presented space. Well suited to homeowners, tenants and landlords preparing a property to move into or hand over.
   Details: [Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning](services.html#move-in-move-out). Enquire: [Book Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning](appointment.html?service=Move-In%20%26%20Move-Out%20Cleaning)

## Neaten Work: cleaning for workplaces

Professional cleaning for offices, shared spaces and managed properties, built around agreed requirements and a consistent approach to presentation.

4. **Office Cleaning**: professional cleaning for offices and working environments, supporting clean, presentable workplaces that create the right impression for employees and visitors. Well suited to offices and working environments that welcome colleagues, clients and visitors.
   Details: [Office Cleaning](services.html#office-cleaning). Enquire: [Book Office Cleaning](appointment.html?service=Office%20Cleaning)

5. **Communal Area Cleaning**: cleaning for shared spaces within residential and commercial properties, keeping them clean, welcoming and well presented. Well suited to entrances, hallways, stairwells and other shared areas in residential and commercial buildings.
   Details: [Communal Area Cleaning](services.html#communal-areas). Enquire: [Book Communal Area Cleaning](appointment.html?service=Communal%20Area%20Cleaning)

6. **Scheduled Commercial Cleaning**: planned cleaning for businesses and managed properties, built around agreed requirements, clear expectations and a consistent approach to workplace presentation. Well suited to businesses and managed properties that need recurring cleaning.
   Details: [Scheduled Commercial Cleaning](services.html#scheduled-commercial). Enquire: [Book Scheduled Commercial Cleaning](appointment.html?service=Scheduled%20Commercial%20Cleaning)

Not sure which service fits? Visitors can choose "Not sure / general enquiry" on the [booking form](appointment.html) and Neaten will help find the right starting point, or [ask a question](contact.html) first.

## Plan builder ("Find your clean")

On the homepage, the [Find your clean](index.html#book-cleaning) tool suggests a starting point from three choices: home or workplace; size (small 1–2 rooms, medium 3–4 rooms, large 5+ rooms, or not sure); and frequency. Suggested plans:

- Weekly: "The Always-Fresh Clean", regular weekly care.
- Fortnightly: "The Balanced Clean", a practical routine balancing schedule and budget.
- One-off: "The Fresh Start Clean", a one-time visit with no ongoing commitment.

These are suggestions to start the conversation, not fixed packages or prices. The tool then continues to the booking enquiry form with the choices filled in.

# Booking a clean

Booking starts with an enquiry, not an instant booking. How it works:

1. **Tell us about your space**: fill in the [booking enquiry form](appointment.html) with the service, property type, property postcode, an optional preferred date and time of day (morning, afternoon or evening), and your name, email and phone number.
2. **We agree the details**: Neaten gets in touch to understand your requirements, confirm availability and agree the price before any cleaning begins.
3. **Enjoy the feeling**: the clean goes ahead as agreed, with clear communication throughout.

Important:
- Submitting an enquiry does **not** confirm an appointment. Requirements, availability and price are agreed first.
- Your requirements and price are confirmed before any cleaning begins, and there is no commitment to book until you're happy.
- Property types on the form: house, flat/apartment, office, communal area, other commercial property, other.
- Prefer to ask first? Use the [contact page](contact.html).
