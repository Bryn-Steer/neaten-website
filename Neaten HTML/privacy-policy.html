Neaten Assistant

Neaten Assistant is an AI-powered helper that answers questions about our services, coverage and booking. To write each reply, the messages in your conversation are sent securely to our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes them under its commercial terms. We use this only to answer your questions.

We don’t keep a record of your conversations. Your conversation is held in your own browser while the tab is open, so it continues as you move between pages, and it is cleared when you close the tab or choose “Start again”. To prevent misuse, we keep a short-lived count of how many questions are asked, linked to a scrambled (hashed) form of your connection’s IP address rather than your messages. These counts are kept only for as long as the limits need them (about a day) and are then routinely deleted.

Please don’t share personal, financial or payment details with the assistant. To arrange a clean or send us your details, please use our booking enquiry form or contact page.
