Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Neaten's approach to domestic and commercial cleaning, the spaces we focus on and the services we're developing for Manchester.

What domestic cleaning services does Neaten offer?
Neaten's domestic cleaning services include regular home cleaning, deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning. Our focus is on helping create comfortable, tidy and well-cared-for homes.
Does Neaten provide commercial cleaning?
Commercial cleaning is a core part of Neaten's proposed service offering. This includes office cleaning, communal-area cleaning and planned cleaning for businesses and managed properties.
Can I arrange regular cleaning?
Regular domestic cleaning and scheduled commercial cleaning are both part of Neaten's service offering. Cleaning requirements, frequency and availability can be discussed when making an enquiry.
How are cleaning requirements agreed?
Every home and workplace has different cleaning needs. Neaten's approach is centred on clear communication and agreed expectations, allowing cleaning requirements to be considered in relation to each individual space.
Which areas does Neaten cover?
Neaten is based in Manchester, with a focus on domestic and commercial cleaning across the local market. Greater Manchester forms part of our intended service area. Please enquire to confirm coverage for your location.
