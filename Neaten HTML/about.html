Our Story

A Better Feeling, In Every Space.

At Neaten, we believe cleaning is about more than appearances. It's about creating homes that feel comfortable, workplaces that make the right impression and spaces that feel properly cared for.

Based in Manchester, Neaten is a domestic and commercial cleaning brand built around thoughtful care, dependable standards and clear communication. Our approach recognises that every environment has different needs, from private homes to offices and shared spaces.

We want cleaning to feel straightforward and reassuring. By focusing on attention to detail, respect for every environment and clear expectations, our aim is to help people feel confident about the care of their spaces.

Our Vision

To build a recognisable and trusted cleaning brand that makes homes feel more comfortable and workplaces more welcoming. We want Neaten to become associated with thoughtful care, dependable service and spaces that feel properly looked after.

Our Mission

  1. Thoughtful Cleaning: To provide domestic and commercial cleaning services with attention to detail, respect for every environment and a considered approach to individual cleaning needs.
  2. Dependable Standards: To establish consistent cleaning standards, clear service expectations and accountability, helping customers feel confident in the care of their homes and workplaces.
  3. Clear Communication: To make the cleaning experience straightforward and reassuring through honest communication, professionalism and a commitment to understanding each customer's requirements.
