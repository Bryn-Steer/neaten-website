Our story

A better feeling,in every space.

At Neaten, we believe cleaning is about more than appearances. It’s about creating homes that feel comfortable, workplaces that make the right impression and spaces that feel properly cared for.

Where we began

Neaten is a Manchester cleaning brand built around thoughtful care, dependable standards and clear communication.

Our approach recognises that every environment has different needs, from private homes to offices and shared spaces.

We want cleaning to feel straightforward and reassuring. By focusing on attention to detail, respect for every environment and clear expectations, our aim is to help people feel confident in the care of the spaces that matter to them.

Vision & mission

What we’reworking towards.

Our vision is to build a recognisable and trusted cleaning brand that makes homes feel more comfortable and workplaces more welcoming — a name associated with thoughtful care, dependable service and spaces that feel properly looked after.

Thoughtful cleaning

To provide domestic and commercial cleaning with attention to detail, respect for every environment and a considered approach to individual cleaning needs.

Dependable standards

To establish consistent cleaning standards, clear service expectations and accountability, helping customers feel confident in the care of their homes and workplaces.

Clear communication

To make the cleaning experience straightforward and reassuring through honest communication, professionalism and a commitment to understanding each customer’s requirements.

What guides us

Five principles,every visit.

These shape how Neaten behaves, communicates and cares for the spaces entrusted to us.

  1. Considered

    Attention to detail and respect for every environment. A thoughtful approach that recognises the importance of the spaces we care for.

  2. Dependable

    Clear communication, consistent standards and a commitment to delivering what has been agreed.

  3. Fresh

    A contemporary, approachable and optimistic outlook, bringing a fresh perspective to domestic and commercial cleaning.

  4. Professional

    A competent, considered approach without unnecessary formality. Clear expectations and straightforward communication at every stage.

  5. Reassuring

    Building confidence through care, respect and accountability, helping customers feel comfortable entrusting us with their spaces.
